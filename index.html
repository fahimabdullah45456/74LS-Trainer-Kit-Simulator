Quick guide
Click a pin, then another pin to add a wire. Right-click a wire to delete it. Power pins and control pins vary by IC: 74LS76 VCC/GND 5/13 (falling-edge clock); 74LS164 14/7 (rising-edge CP, active-LOW CLR); 74LS165 16/8 (rising-edge gated clock, active-LOW SH/LD); DM74LS181N 24/12 (combinational ALU; active-HIGH Cn carry-in and Cn+4 carry-out). Pin colours: green = HIGH, grey = LOW, black = floating, orange = unknown/conflict. Use ▣ to choose an IC, then click a base.
